# Article for infinite product with [signature](../product_signature.html) 4:(-1,1,-1,0).


$$ F(q) := \sum_{n=0}^\infty r(n)q^n := \frac{f(-q,-q^2)}{\varphi(-q^2)} = {(q;q^2)_\infty}{(-q^2;q^2)_\infty} $$

## Combinatorial interpretation of product
$(-1)^n r(n)$ is the number of partitions of $n$ into distinct parts.

## [Asymptotic formula](../asymptotics.html)

$ \displaystyle (-1)^n r(n) \sim \frac{e^{\frac{\pi  \sqrt{n}}{\sqrt{3}}}}{4 \sqrt[4]{3} n^{3/4}} $ as $ n\to\infty. $

## $q$-series representations
